## typography
- High punctuation by profile: [fr-FR] narrow no-break space (U+202F) before ; ! ?, no-break space before :, no-break spaces inside « »; [fr-CA] no space (or a thin one) before ; ! ?, no-break space before : and inside « »; [fr-CH] traditionally no space before ; ! ?; [fr/eu] no space before : ; ! ? and none inside « ». Never a space before , . … ). Numeric times (13:52) take none. In Automatic, follow the document and flag mixing. (M) [FR-M01, FR-M02; OQLF; fr.wikipedia (summarised); EU-ISG]
- « » are the main quotes; nested quotes “ ” (OQLF) or « » (house choice); the same spacing with straight quotes. (M) [FR-M03]
- A full quoted sentence introduced by a colon and starting with a capital keeps its final mark inside « » and takes no period after: Il me dit : « À partir de demain, ce sera difficile. » A blended quotation takes the period outside: Il me dit que, « à partir de demain, ce sera difficile ». Exception: a quotation woven into the sentence that ends with a complete sentence takes the period after ». (M flag) [FR-M04; OQLF; EU-ISG]
- Ellipsis attached to the previous word with a space after; no period after it; not "etc.…". (M) [FR-M05]
- Spaced en dash for asides (OQLF: space before the opening dash, no-break space after it; no-break space before the closing dash, space after); unspaced en dash inside compound place names (Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean). (M) [FR-M12]
- Typographic apostrophe ’ preferred, no space after it; no no-break space between an apostrophe and « (d'« huile »). (M setting) [FR-M02, FR-M15]
- Accents, diaeresis and cedilla stay on capitals, À included; œ and æ are part of the spelling (cœur, œuvre, ex æquo). (M) [FR-M09, FR-M10; Académie]

## numbers
- Decimal comma; thousands grouped with a no-break space (12 535,75); years and codes not grouped. (M) [FR-M06]
- No-break space between a number and %, a unit or a currency symbol (8 %, 58 kg, 37 °C, 25,75 €); currency after the amount; times in words 14 h 30. (M) [FR-M07]
- Ordinals 1er, 1re, 1ers, 1res, 2e, 2es, 2d, 2de; never -ème, -ième, -me or -è (except Nième, Xième); raised letters stay lower case. (M) [FR-M08; OQLF]

## words
- Courtesy titles M. (never Mr), MM., Mme, Mlle, Dr, Me; no final period when the abbreviation ends with the word's last letter. (M) [FR-M11]
- Euphonic t with hyphens: a-t-il (never a-t'il). (M) [FR-M13]
- The 1990 rectifications are recommended, not required: never flag either spelling under the default (traditional, rectified or both). (guard) [FR-M14; Rectifications 1990]
- Official French terms instead of anglicisms where one exists (FranceTerme data, Licence Ouverte), suggested only in formal or administrative text; mandatory only for State bodies. (S) [FR-S03; FranceTerme]
- Institutions (France): l'Assemblée nationale, le Conseil d'État, le ministère de la Culture. (S) [FR-S01, unverified]

## dialogue
- « at the first reply and » at the last are optional; each change of speaker starts with a dash (en dash with a space per OQLF examples, or em dash as in many books; one style per document). A multi-paragraph reply inside guillemets reopens each later paragraph with « (some books use »). (M) [OQLF; FR-S02]
- A short incise (dit-il, demanda-t-il) stays inside the guillemets; a long incise closes and reopens them; inverted incises take hyphens and the euphonic -t-. (M) [OQLF *Guillemets et citation*]

## register
- Inclusive writing by policy, in order: epicene and collective nouns, then full doublets, then feminised titles (la ministre); abbreviated doublets only in tables, forms or microblogging, with ( ) or [ ] (OQLF doesn't retain . · / - or capitals); point médian only on explicit opt-in, with warnings: banned in texts for the Journal officiel (2017 circular) and in teaching (2021 circular, upheld by the Conseil d'État on 20 Dec 2024), accepted on Paris plaques (CE 31 Dec 2025), no statute bans it (PPL n° 282 pending). (S) [FR-S04; OQLF; Circulaire 2017; CE 2024]

## letters
- Letters: Madame, / Monsieur, ; "Cordialement" in email; a full formule de politesse in formal letters (convention). (S) [convention]
