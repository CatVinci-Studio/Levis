# Weekend Reading Club: first-year report

The city library started the Weekend Reading Club in January to bring more teenagers into the library outside school hours.

## Method

Over the course of the whole year, from January all the way through to December, the library staff kept careful records of attendance at every single session, and in addition to that they also handed out a short paper questionnaire at the end of each month in order to find out what the participants themselves actually thought about the club and about the way it was run.

## Findings

Attendance grew from 120 registered participants in January to 310 in December. The most common complaint was that the library closes at 4 p.m. on Sundays, which cuts the Sunday session short.

Next year the club will add a monthly author visit.
