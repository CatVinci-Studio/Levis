# Q3 support review

## Summary

The support team closed 2,310 tickets in Q3, up from 1,980 in Q2. Median first response time fell to 3.1 hours. The main driver was the new triage rota introduced in August.

## Tickets by channel

| Channel | July | August | September | Total |
| ------- | ---- | ------ | --------- | ----- |
| Email   | 410  | 395    | 380       | 1,185 |
| Chat    | 220  | 260    | 300       | 780   |
| Phone   | 120  | 110    | 105       | 335   |

## Next quarter

We plan to move phone support to a callback model and to publish the first set of help-centre articles in November.
