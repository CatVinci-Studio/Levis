# Our new reading room

In today's fast-paced world, the new reading room is not just a space — it's a testament to our unwavering commitment to learning. Opened on 3 March with 48 seats, it serves as a vibrant hub where students can delve into a rich tapestry of resources. Moreover, the room boasts seamless Wi-Fi, quiet booths and a printing corner. In conclusion, it stands as a pivotal step forward for our community.

Librarian Ana Ruiz said: "We moved 2,000 books here in one week."
