# The last bookshop on Mill Street

When I was eleven, my grandfather took me to the bookshop on Mill Street every Saturday morning. It was a narrow place with a creaky floor, and the owner, Mr. Hale, kept a kettle on a hot plate behind the counter.

It was a very special place that meant a lot to me. At the end of the day, it was where I learned that reading could be a way of being with someone without having to talk. My grandfather read history; I read anything with a map on the first page.

The shop closed in 2019. These days there is a phone repair stall where the counter used to be, and every time I walk past, I think about how times have changed and how nothing lasts forever.
