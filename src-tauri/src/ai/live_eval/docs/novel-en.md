# Chapter 7

The rain had not stopped since morning. Elena stood under the station awning with her collar turned up, watching the departures board flick from one delay to the next.

"You're late," she said angrily when Tom finally appeared.

"The bus broke down," he replied apologetically. "I ran the last mile."

"You could have called," she said coldly.

He held up his phone. The screen was black. "Battery," he said sheepishly.
